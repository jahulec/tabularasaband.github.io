17 October 2026Band

Festivals and awards. Our 2026 so far

A look back at the festival stages and awards that became part of our story in 2026.

Club shows are one part of our journey. Festivals and competitions are another, often introducing our music to people hearing us for the first time.

In 2026 we took 1st place at Ryk Silnika, Pułtusk i Muzyka, Music On in Kalisz Pomorski, Rockowa Przystań, RoCK Festiwal in Kołobrzeg, Kozy Nostra Rock Fest and Rock Battle of Barcja.

Thank you to the organisers, juries, technical crews and everyone who stopped by the stage. Visit Press for more of our milestones and the gallery for live photos.
